The CPI climbed steadily.

Consumer Price Index, Australia

The Consumer Price Index tracks the price level of a broad basket of goods and services. A rising CPI means the same money buys less — and across the decade the climb was steady and relentless rather than sudden.

State CPI Comparison

A map makes the regional pattern easier to compare. Hover any state or territory to inspect its figure — darker blue means higher CPI pressure. Inflation was felt everywhere, but not evenly.

01 · 04

The real pain appears when wages lag prices.

Wage Price Index versus Inflation
The shaded region marks the cost-of-living shock, where inflation rose much faster than wage growth.

When inflation rises faster than wage growth, real purchasing power falls. The 2022 to 2023 period shows the clearest gap: prices sprinted while pay packets walked, so even workers with a pay rise effectively went backwards.

Can we still afford our lifestyle in 2025?

Beyond the weekly shop, the small luxuries crept up too. Streaming subscriptions and the humble Big Mac — a global yardstick for everyday prices — both climbed far faster than the broad rate of inflation over the decade.
